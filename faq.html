Frequently Asked Questions
Is this website completely free?
+
Yes. CS2-foryou is entirely free to use. We built this platform as a training ground for the community to experience the thrill of case opening without the financial risk.
Can I withdraw the skins I win to my Steam account?
+
No. This is strictly a simulation website. The skins you unbox exist only within your virtual inventory on our site to track your theoretical luck and value. No real items or currency are awarded.
Are the case odds accurate to the real game?
+
Absolutely. We use the official, disclosed drop rates for Counter-Strike 2. You have exactly the same chance (0.26%) to unbox a rare knife or glove here as you do in the actual game.
Will my inventory be saved if I leave the site?
+
Currently, your inventory is saved locally in your browser session. If you clear your cache, your simulated inventory will reset. We are working on a login feature to permanently save your virtual collections!
